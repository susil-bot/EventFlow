# PosterCard

Discover card: a tall cover (uploaded image or generated art) with a date
strip ("Sun, 15 Nov", plus "onwards" for multi-session events), then the
title, place, category and format, and the price.

- Sold out, few places left and canceled show as a tag on the poster.
- The title link stretches over the whole card, so each card is one link.
- Distance ("290 km away") appears when Discover is filtered by a city.
